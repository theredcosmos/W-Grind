Array and ArrayList

Contiguous memory. Index access is instant, inserting or removing in the middle shifts everything after it. An ArrayList is an array that grows by half when it fills.

          HashMap and HashSet

          A key goes through hashCode, gets spread, and lands in bucket hash & (n-1). Keys that share a bucket form a chain. When the table passes 75% full it doubles and every entry is rehashed. A HashSet is a HashMap whose values are a dummy object.
